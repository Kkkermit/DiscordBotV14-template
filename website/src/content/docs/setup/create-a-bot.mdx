Every Discord bot belongs to an **application** in the Discord Developer Portal. Here you create one, get the bot's
**token** (its password), and invite the bot to your server.

## Create the application

<Steps>

1. Open the [Discord Developer Portal](https://discord.com/developers/applications) and log in.

2. Click **New Application**, give it a name (this becomes your bot's name), accept the terms and click **Create**.

3. Optional: on the **General Information** page, add an app icon and a description. They're shown on the bot's
   profile.

</Steps>

## Get the bot token

<Steps>

1. Open the **Bot** page in the sidebar.

2. Click **Reset Token**, confirm, and then click **Copy**. Paste the token somewhere safe for now: you'll need it in
   [Configure your bot](../configuration/).

</Steps>

:::danger[Keep your token secret]
Anyone who has your token can control your bot. Never share it, post it in Discord or commit it to GitHub. If it
leaks, click **Reset Token** straight away: the old token stops working immediately.
:::

## Enable the Message Content intent

Still on the **Bot** page, scroll to **Privileged Gateway Intents**, switch on **Message Content Intent** and click
**Save Changes**.

The bot needs it to read messages, which is how prefix commands such as `?ping` work. Slash commands work without it.
The template doesn't need the other privileged intents (Presence and Server Members).

## Invite the bot to your server

<Steps>

1. Open the **OAuth2** page in the sidebar and scroll to **OAuth2 URL Generator**.

2. Under **Scopes**, tick `bot` and `applications.commands`. The second one lets the bot add slash commands.

3. Under **Bot Permissions**, tick:

   - View Channels
   - Send Messages
   - Send Messages in Threads
   - Embed Links
   - Read Message History

   That's everything the bundled commands need. Add more only when your own features need them, and avoid
   **Administrator**.

4. Copy the **Generated URL** at the bottom, open it in your browser, choose your server and click **Authorize**.

</Steps>

The bot now appears in your server's member list, offline until you start it.

:::tip[Prefer a ready-made link?]
This link asks for the same permissions. Replace `YOUR_CLIENT_ID` with the **Application ID** from the **General
Information** page:

```text
https://discord.com/oauth2/authorize?client_id=YOUR_CLIENT_ID&scope=bot+applications.commands&permissions=274877991936
```

:::

## Copy your user ID

The template has **developer-only** commands that only you can use. To mark yourself as the developer, you need your
Discord user ID. With **Developer Mode** turned on (**User Settings → Advanced**), right-click your name in Discord and
choose **Copy User ID**. It's a long number, like `123456789012345678`.

## Checklist

- [ ] You copied your bot token and kept it private
- [ ] **Message Content Intent** is on
- [ ] The bot is in your server
- [ ] You copied your user ID (optional, for developer-only commands)
